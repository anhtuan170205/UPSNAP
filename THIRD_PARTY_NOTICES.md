# Third-party notices

## Lightroom-Presets

UPSNAP's browser adaptations of Kodak Portra 400, Kodak Gold 200, Fujifilm
Classic Chrome, Ilford HP5 Plus, and CineStill 800T are derived from the
settings and artistic direction in
[peva3/Lightroom-Presets](https://github.com/peva3/Lightroom-Presets), pinned
to commit `f22f4d8057aaaada6df3f0ed7fbe6b952a30db77`.

Source files:

- `Presets/Color-Negative/Kodak Portra 400.xmp`
- `Presets/Color-Negative/Kodak Gold 200.xmp`
- `Presets/Color-Negative/Fujifilm Classic Chrome.xmp`
- `Presets/Black-White/Ilford HP5 Plus.xmp`
- `Presets/Creative/Cinestill 800T.xmp`

The XMP files target Adobe Lightroom's RAW pipeline. UPSNAP does not execute
or distribute those files; it applies documented browser adaptations to
already-rendered, local webcam images.
